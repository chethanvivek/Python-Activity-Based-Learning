# Assumptions – Employee Payroll and Deduction Analysis

1. Basic pay, allowances, incentives, overtime hours, and overtime rate are entered by the user.
2. Overtime pay is calculated by multiplying overtime hours by the overtime rate.
3. Gross salary is calculated by adding basic pay, allowances, overtime pay, and incentives.
4. The following progressive deduction slabs are assumed:
   - First ₹10,000: No deduction.
   - Next ₹10,000: 5% deduction.
   - Amount above ₹20,000: 10% deduction.

5. Each deduction rate is applied only to the amount within its respective slab.
6. Net salary is calculated by subtracting total deductions from gross salary.
7. All salary amounts must be zero or positive.
8. These deduction rates are illustrative and are not actual government tax rules.
