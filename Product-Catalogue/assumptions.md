# Assumptions

1. The program analyses 3 products.
2. Each product contains a product ID, price, and stock quantity.
3. Product price must be greater than zero.
4. Stock quantity must be zero or greater.
5. A product record is invalid if its price is zero or negative, or its stock quantity is negative.
6. A product requires replenishment if its stock quantity is 5 or less.
7. Inventory value = Product Price × Stock Quantity.
8. Invalid product records are excluded from the total inventory value.
9. Product IDs are entered as text.
10. The program assumes that the user enters valid numeric prices and stock quantities.
