# Conclusion

The Attendance Recovery Computation program successfully calculates
a student's current attendance percentage and determines the minimum
number of upcoming classes required to reach the 75% eligibility
threshold.

It evaluates three future attendance scenarios: 100%, 90%, and 80%.
The program also identifies situations where the student is already
eligible or cannot reach the required attendance under a particular
scenario.

This case study helped in understanding variables, arithmetic
operators, conditional statements, loops, lists, and basic
problem-solving techniques in Python.
