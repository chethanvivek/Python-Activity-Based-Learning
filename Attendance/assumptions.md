# Assumptions

1. The minimum attendance eligibility requirement is 75%.
2. The number of classes attended cannot be negative.
3. The total number of classes conducted must be greater than zero.
4. Classes attended cannot exceed total classes conducted.
5. Three future attendance scenarios are considered:
   - Scenario 1: 100% attendance
   - Scenario 2: 90% attendance
   - Scenario 3: 80% attendance
6. Future classes are counted one at a time.
7. The number of attended future classes is rounded down to a whole
   number when calculating each scenario.
8. A scenario with an attendance rate less than or equal to the
   eligibility threshold cannot improve a student who is below the
   threshold to the required percentage.
9. The eligibility threshold is fixed at 75%.
